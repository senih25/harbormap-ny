# Help and support

**HarborMap NY is an independent prototype, not a service provider, government hotline or immigration legal advisor.**

To find services, visit [HarborMap NY](https://senih25.github.io/harbormap-ny/) and verify each facility directly with the agency listed.

- Immediate danger in the United States: **911**.
- Emotional crisis support in the United States: **988** (call/text).
- NYC public service information: **311** within NYC.
- New York State community referrals: **211** (regional availability and provider details vary).
- Immigration legal help in NYC: [Mayor's Office of Immigrant Affairs legal resources](https://www.nyc.gov/site/immigrants/legal-resources/moia-immigration-legal-support-centers.page).

For errors in this website, use the guided [public issues](https://github.com/senih25/harbormap-ny/issues). For sensitive technical vulnerabilities use [private security reporting](https://github.com/senih25/harbormap-ny/security/advisories/new). Do not publish immigration records, identity documents, residential addresses, health data, hidden shelter locations or personal emergency details.

The repository cannot provide program eligibility determinations, personal advice, emergency dispatch or guaranteed response times.
