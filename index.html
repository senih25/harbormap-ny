04 / OUR PROMISE

Open data. Clear limits.

HarborMap NY is an independent community prototype, not a government agency or emergency service. Information may be outdated or incomplete. Always confirm access, hours and services with the provider. Not legal, medical or eligibility advice. NYC launch coverage only; New York State expansion is planned.

No accounts, ads or analytics. Search and approximate distances run in your browser. Precise location is requested only if you select “Near me”, is not stored, and is not sent to HarborMap servers. External maps and websites have their own privacy policies.
