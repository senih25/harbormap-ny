ABOUT / HARBORMAP NY

Good directions start with good information.

HarborMap NY helps New Yorkers discover publicly listed services using verifiable, dated public data. It is an independent community prototype — not a City of New York, New York State or MTA product.

What this directory includes

Our launch map covers New York City only. It includes publicly listed hospitals from the New York State Department of Health, NYC public computer centers and Queens Public Library branches. The underlying datasets were checked on October 19, 2026. Counts describe valid imported records, not verified live availability.

Original data, original agencies

Every mapped facility is linked to the agency source record or appropriate original provider. More detailed provenance is in our machine-readable source register.

  • Queens Library Branches — Queens Public Library ↗. Location coordinates are rounded, so use the address for navigation.
  • Citywide Public Computer Centers — NYC OTI ↗. Administrative “Currently open” status is not a real-time opening indicator. Some facilities restrict access by age or membership; confirm before visiting.
  • Health Facility General Information — NYS DOH ↗. The app includes records classified as HOSP (Hospital) in five NYC counties; it does not include all clinics or pharmacies. Hospital geocoding is source-provided and may be imperfect.

We follow NYC Open Data terms and OPEN-NY terms subject to any additional source-specific restrictions. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Original agencies do not endorse this app.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to create an account or disclose my immigration status?

No. HarborMap NY has no registration and does not request immigration status or identity documents.

Are facilities open right now?

We cannot confirm live hours. Updates vary by agency, and public computer center status is administrative rather than real time. Please call or check the original provider website before visiting.

Does HarborMap NY give legal or medical advice?

No. It links to NYC MOIA legal support, NYC Health + Hospitals, NYC311 and 211 NY. Providers confirm their services, availability and eligibility.

Does the map cover all of New York State?

No. The map currently covers NYC. 211 New York offers a statewide referral directory, but its records are not ingested here. Statewide facility expansion will require fresh, separately verified data and rights review.

Can I find a pharmacy or live transit arrival?

Not in the mapped facility records. We link to the official MTA website for transit and to state professional-license verification; neither is represented as a live pharmacy finder or transport feed.

Your privacy

No account, cookies for user tracking, advertisement network, server-side analytics, or behavioral profiles are built into this application. Search, filters and distance calculations happen in your browser. Only pressing Near me requests a location through your browser; it is kept in volatile memory, not stored in localStorage or transmitted to HarborMap's application server.

Loading the map does contact OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which may see your IP address and the approximate area displayed. Clicking directions, phone links, or external directories may contact those third parties. No automatic geolocation is requested. You can still use the text list without granting location permission.

Safety and feedback

For immediate danger in the United States, call 911. For US emotional crisis support, call or text 988. For other NYC services call 311; for statewide resource referrals call 211. HarborMap is not a hotline, emergency dispatcher, legal advisor or healthcare provider.

To report inaccurate facility records, use the original dataset links above. For HarborMap software feedback, visit the project issue tracker. Do not post sensitive personal details, health records or immigration documents in a public issue.
